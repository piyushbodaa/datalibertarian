How we work

Methodology & disclaimer

Babuwatch publishes only what a court decided, attributed to that court, and says exactly how far each record has been checked.

Public statement

Babuwatch documents court-adjudicated findings against public servants in India, drawn from court judgments and from the official conviction records that anti-corruption and vigilance agencies publish. Every finding is the court's, not ours. Inclusion records what a court decided on the date shown; it does not establish anything beyond that finding, and a trial-court conviction may since have been appealed. Records are corrected or withdrawn as facts develop. We withhold names by default and never publish the identities of complainants, victims, witnesses or minors.

One method, several registers

Babuwatch and its sub-registers

Babuwatch lists every published record. Each record also belongs to exactly one register, which hosts its page: police officers belong to Copwatch India, whose methodology governs police records; civil servants belong to Babuwatch itself, under the rules below. Further registers (for example, for a single service) will follow the same method.
