What Babuwatch does

One register of what the courts decided about public servants

Record

We record convictions and adverse court findings against public servants of every government in India — Union, state and local — and cite the court and the source for each one.

Attribute

Every finding belongs to the court that made it. We add no findings of our own, and we say plainly how far each record has been verified.

Follow up

Trial convictions can be appealed. Each record shows its appeal status as known from the sources, and convictions later set aside leave the counts.

Protect

Names are withheld unless a two-key naming gate clears them. Complainants, victims, witnesses and minors are never named.

Babuwatch is a family of registers under one method. Police officers are covered by Copwatch India; civil servants of departments, municipal bodies and other government offices are covered here. Every record passes the same verification and naming rules, whichever register it lives in.

Reporting corruption

Babuwatch is a record, not a complaints desk

Where complaints go

Corruption by a public servant is reported to the authorities, not to us: your state's Anti-Corruption Bureau or Vigilance department, the Lokayukta where one exists, the Central Bureau of Investigation for Union government employees, or the Central Vigilance Commission. Contacting Babuwatch does not notify any authority or begin any case.
