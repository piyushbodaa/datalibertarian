Victimless crimes

No victim, no crime.

India convicts its own people for what they eat, drink, smoke, wager, or do with their own bodies — acts with no complaining victim. This register will document those convictions: prostitution, gambling, alcohol, beef and food prohibitions, cigarettes, e-cigarettes and vapes, and the rest — each one attributed to the court that decided it.

Methodology

What qualifies, and how we verify

The standard

A record qualifies when a court has convicted or adversely found against a person for a consensual act with no complaining victim — under the statutes named in the categories above or any equivalent state law. Full methodology, verification levels and the naming rule will be published here before the first record goes live; the standard follows the Babuwatch method: record, attribute, correct — and withhold names unless a naming gate clears them. MethodologyAbout usContact
Victimless Crimes records court findings in the public interest. Each finding is attributed to the court that made it; inclusion establishes nothing beyond that finding, and convictions may be under appeal. Names are withheld unless a naming gate clears them, and records are corrected or withdrawn as facts develop.
