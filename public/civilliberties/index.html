Civil liberties

Freedom is the point.

Indians are punished — arrested, charged, convicted — for exercising the freedoms the Constitution promises: speaking, believing, worshipping, assembling. This register will name those victims of state overreach, starting with freedom of speech and freedom of religion, with more sections to be added.

Methodology

What qualifies, and how we verify

The standard

A record qualifies when a person has been arrested, charged or convicted for exercising a basic freedom — speech, religion, and the further sections once announced. Full methodology, verification levels and the naming rule will be published here before the first record goes live; the standard follows the Babuwatch method: record, attribute, correct — and withhold names unless a naming gate clears them. MethodologyAbout usContact
Civil Liberties records state action against free people in the public interest. Each finding is attributed to the court or authority that made it; inclusion establishes nothing beyond that finding, and convictions may be under appeal. Names are withheld unless a naming gate clears them, and records are corrected or withdrawn as facts develop.
