Sin

0 records — convictions involving activities commonly criminalised as “vice”: gambling, alcohol, cannabis and LSD, sex-work-related offences, and cattle/meat-related conduct. The register is under construction; no case has yet been verified to the publication standard. An empty register states what we have checked and not found — it is never filled with unverified cases.

Why an empty page

This register will list only convictions: a court's explicit finding of guilt for the specific conduct, verified against the judgment itself. No candidate has yet cleared that bar, so the count is 0. We do not publish allegations, arrests, seizures, charge sheets or pending prosecutions as convictions — they may be recorded internally as leads, and they never appear in these counts.

What will be recorded — the exact conduct, not a label

When a case qualifies, the register states what the court convicted the person of doing, in that jurisdiction, under the law as it stood. A transport offence is never described as “convicted for drinking”; a slaughter offence is never described as “convicted for eating beef”. Where the law has changed since, the record shows the law as it stood at conviction and its current status separately — a later change does not make a historical conviction disappear.

What we will never do

Assume a category is unlawful everywhere — each record names the jurisdiction and law actually applied. Treat an unknown as known: age, consent, substance, quantity or species that the source does not establish stay unknown. Publish private individuals' names, addresses or identity details by default — entries for private persons are unnamed unless a court record requires otherwise. “Sin” is the project's editorial name for this category of criminalised conduct, not a moral judgment about the people convicted.

Inclusion policy

A record appears here when the shared evidence gate confirms: a preserved source, an authenticated court, an explicit conviction for the defendant, a case reference, a V2/V3 verification decision, and a classified conduct in one of the five categories above. Mixed offences are shown with their full context. Sources of candidates: official judgments and official registers — news reports are leads only.

Corrections

If a record here is wrong, write to the project and it will be corrected or removed. Retracted records are deleted, not quietly kept.

These registers list only people a court convicted — not anyone merely accused, charge-sheeted, suspended or acquitted.
