How this is built

Methodology

How records get here, and what keeps them out.

The one rule

Only convictions. A person appears here because a court found them guilty.

What this is

Babuwatch compiles court convictions of Indian public servants — police officers and civil servants alike. Every record states the court that convicted, the case number or citation, and links to the source.

What is included

Only convictions. We do not list anyone merely accused, charge-sheeted, suspended or acquitted, and a departmental action is not a conviction. An adverse finding or a compensation order is documented on Copwatch India, not counted here as a conviction. A conviction later set aside on appeal leaves the active list; its history is retained internally.

The evidence gate

Every input — High Court and Supreme Court records, trial-court convictions, and the civil-servant register — passes one shared gate before publication. The gate requires: a preserved source, an authenticated court, an explicit conviction for the defendant, a case reference, a verification decision of V2 (source authenticated and attributed) or V3 (material fact corroborated), and a supported public-servant role. A record that fails any check is withheld and counted, never published.

This build: 1,122 High Court / Supreme Court records screened → 275 convictions published; 883 trial-court convictions screened → 407 published (67 already covered above); 161 civil-servant register entries screened → 0 published. The full machine-readable report ships with every release at /babuwatch/data/gate-report.json.

Numbers

Every figure comes from a named official source. Where a figure could not be sourced it is left blank rather than estimated. A blank is a finding: we looked and did not find, which is not the same as zero. Counts of convictions are never inflated by merging different defendants or adding appeals as new cases.

Verification

An extracted record is re-checked by a separate reviewer against the document it cites before publication. If the claim cannot be found in its own cited source, the record is withheld rather than published. Records awaiting verification are held internally — they are not lost, and they will appear here if they clear the gate.

Corrections

If a record here is wrong — overturned on appeal, acquitted, or a misread judgment — tell us and it will be corrected or removed. Retracted records are deleted, not quietly kept.

Babuwatch is a register of convictions. It lists only people a court convicted — not anyone merely accused, charge-sheeted, suspended or acquitted.
