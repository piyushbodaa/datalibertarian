How this is built

Methodology

How records get here, and what keeps them out.

The one rule

Only convictions. A person appears here because a court found them guilty.

What this is

Babuwatch compiles court convictions of Indian public servants — police officers and civil servants alike. Every record states the court that convicted, the case number where one exists, and links to the source.

What is included

Only convictions. We do not list anyone merely accused, charge-sheeted, suspended or acquitted, and a departmental action is not a conviction.

Numbers

Every figure comes from a named official source. Where a figure could not be sourced it is left blank rather than estimated. A blank is a finding: we looked and did not find, which is not the same as zero.

Verification

An extracted record is re-checked by a separate reviewer against the document it cites before publication. If the number cannot be found in its own cited source, the record is withheld rather than published.

Corrections

If a record here is wrong — overturned on appeal, acquitted, or a misread judgment — tell us and it will be corrected or removed. Retracted records are deleted, not quietly kept.

Babuwatch is a register of convictions. It lists only people a court convicted — not anyone merely accused, charge-sheeted, suspended or acquitted.
